## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- **Cyrius port (2.0.0-dev), M0 Part 1**: bijli is now a Cyrius project. `cyrius port` (6.7.18)
  moved the Rust crate into `rust-old/` as the frozen 1.2.0 oracle, byte for byte, and scaffolded
  `cyrius.cyml`, `src/main.cyr` and the test, bench and fuzz stubs. The scaffold's overwrites are
  repaired:
  - CLAUDE.md is the Cyrius version (port plan P6), and the roadmap is restored;
  - `docs/development/state.md` is new;
  - CI runs `build`, `test`, `test --bounds`, `deps --locked`, `fuzz` and `audit`, and the Rust
    workflows move to `rust-old/.github/workflows/`;
  - `cyrius.cyml` reads `${file:VERSION}` and has `bench` in `[test] stdlib`;
  - the Rust-only scripts and the Rust architecture overview move to `rust-old/`.

  ADR 0001 (the port) is accepted. ADR 0002 (the 2.0 API changes: removed deprecations,
  fixed-size grids, fallible constructors, error codes with a last-error record, one time
  convention e^{+jωt} with IEEE handedness, cross-target bit identity, a transport error) is new.
- **Cyrius port, M0 Part 2a**: the golden-value generator `scripts/oracle/` drives the frozen
  oracle and writes `tests/golden/constants.tsv` (12 rows), `tests/golden/math.tsv` (34,734 rows)
  and the generated `src/constants.cyr`, deterministically, with a `--check` mode and a glibc
  provenance check. `scripts/oracle/validate.py` re-derives every row independently (0
  disagreements). It found that Rust std's `f64::cbrt` is compiler-builtins' correctly rounded
  `cbrt`, not glibc's, which the port plan's P3 record now says.
- **Cyrius port, M0 Part 2b**: `src/mathx.cyr` (Rust's NaN-ignoring min/max/clamp, the predicates,
  copysign, the folded degree constants, compiler-rt's `__powidf2`); the `[lib]` bundle
  `dist/bijli.cyr`; the parity harness `tests/parity.tcyr` (15,507 rows exact, 0 failed, the class-C
  rows measured until `src/crmath.cyr`); and the port tooling, run in CI: surface parity, the
  Rust-test ledger (`docs/development/rust-test-ledger.md`), the benchmark record
  (`docs/benchmarks-rust-v-cyrius.md`) and the doc-example check.
- **Cyrius port, M0 Part 3 — M0 done**: the cross-target baselines (`docs/development/port-parity.md`): the corpus
  replayed on x86_64 ELF, x86_64 PE and aarch64, with per-function digests in the harness (20 of 25 functions
  bit-identical on all three), the measured class-C histograms, and the size of M1's corrected oracle (125 rows where
  glibc misrounds). Probes p7–p14 in `scripts/probes/` (the language-review probes moved there): grid memory, struct
  vs f64 hot loops, callbacks, the FDTD kernel (bit-identical to Rust's), Rust's `.sum()`, Touchstone text, the
  cell-step cost and the last-error record.
- **Cyrius port, M1 part 1a**: correctly rounded exp, sin, cos, sin_cos, hypot, pow and atan2 (`src/crmath.cyr`, impetus's,
  with its generated tables `src/crmath_data.cyr` and `scripts/oracle/crtables.py`), wrapped in `src/mathx.cyr`, plus
  atan as atan2(x, 1). The corrected oracle `tests/golden/math.cr.tsv` (125 rows where glibc misrounds) and
  `scripts/oracle/cr_verify.py`, which checks the primitives against MPFR beyond the corpus (700,000 calls on x86_64
  and aarch64, all equal). Those functions' 14,766 golden rows (cbrt's included) now gate at 0 ulp, on all three targets.
- **Cyrius port, M1 part 1b**: correctly rounded acos and log10 (`src/crmath_k.cyr`, kimiya's, with its ln and atan
  tables generated by `scripts/oracle/crtables_k.py` into `src/crmath_k_data.cyr`) and asin, bijli's, on the same atan
  core. `cr_verify.py` checks asin on 1,000,000 arguments and acos and log10 on 300,000 each against MPFR, on x86_64

  aarch64 (`docs/development/port-parity.md` §6).
- **Cyrius port, M1 part 1c — the math primitives complete**: correctly rounded tan, bijli's, on impetus's sin and cos
  phases (`src/crmath.cyr`'s tan section). sin and cos stay unrounded, are divided as double-doubles with proven
  bounds, and fall back to a fixed-point slow path; the quotient is rounded once. `cr_verify.py` checks tan on
  1,000,000 arguments and the slow path alone on 157,132 more against MPFR, on x86_64 and aarch64: all equal.
  `tests/crmath_bijli.tcyr` tests bijli's own tan and asin (33 assertions). Every one of the corpus's 34,746 rows now
  gates, none measured or pending, on all three targets (`docs/development/port-parity.md` §7).
- **Cyrius port, M1 part 2a — fmtf, log, error**:
  - `src/fmtf.cyr`: impetus's exact f64 printer, Rust's Display and serde_json's form, into a bounded buffer, pinned

  - `src/error.cyr`: the `BJ_ERR_*` codes and the per-thread last-error record. Each error keeps its variant's fields

  - The bundle is `private`: consumers see exactly its `public` items, the constants now among them.
  - Tests include `tests/lib.cyr` instead of `[test] modules`, which folded the library into each test's own file and

  - error.rs's 10 and logging.rs's 1 Rust tests are ported. 73,832 rows pass on all three targets, with equal

- **Cyrius port, M1 part 3a — field, first part**: `src/field.cyr` with `BjFieldVector` (by value, its operators and

- **Cyrius port, M1 part 3c — field complete**: `bj_trace_field_line`, Rust's forward-Euler field-line tracer. Its field

- **Cyrius port, M1 part 3d — maxwell and charge**: `src/maxwell.cyr` (the divergences, the displacement current,

- **Cyrius port, M1 part 4 — the JSON of `BjComplex`, `BjFieldVector` and `BjPointCharge`** (port plan D5, amended):
  `src/json.cyr`. The writers (`Bj<T>_to_json(p, sb)`) append serde_json's document, every f64 through bayan's
  printer (the derive's), `null` for NaN and ±∞. They are hand-written because a `#derive(Serialize)` in a second
  `private` file fails to compile on 6.7.18 (probe p16b, filed). The readers (`Bj<T>_from_json(buf, len, out)`)
  apply serde's derive semantics on bayan's value tree: object or sequence form, unknown keys skipped, serde's
  reasons, byte offsets, `out` untouched on an error. A per-read decode region on freelist chunks keeps the heaps
  unchanged, and reads are thread-safe. A pre-pass corrects bayan where it reads otherwise than serde_json (probe
  p16, filed): it keeps the sign of `-0`, reads integers past i64, and refuses raw control characters and non-UTF-8
  bytes in strings. New: `BJ_ERR_DESERIALIZE` (ADR 0002 row 10), `json.tsv` (14,758 rows) and `json.cr.tsv`, with a
  `validate.py` model of serde. Surface parity now requires the JSON pair for every serde type.

- **Cyrius port, M1 part 5c-1 — BjComplex's range edges** (ADR 0002 rows 14, 15). Where a part leaves
  [2^-511, 2^511), hisab's chain runs with an unbounded exponent and rounds its last step once. This covers `abs`,
  `norm_sq`, the product, `inv`, the quotient, `is_zero` and `sqrt`, and `ln` and `powf` wherever the unbounded |z|

  `complex.adr.tsv` (1,592 rows) and proves, row by row, that in-range rows keep hisab's bits.

- **Cyrius port, M1 part 5c-2 — BjComplex's transcendental range edges** (ADR 0002 row 16). The unbounded chain now
  covers its transcendental steps too: e^re in `exp`, cosh and sinh in `sin` and `cos`, ln and pow of an |z| or a
  power past DBL_MAX or below 2^-1022, and the angles of `sqrt` and `powf`. Each is taken to 53 bits with an
  unbounded exponent and each final product rounded once. The new `src/crmath_ux.cyr` computes them on crmath's

  against MPFR at 53 bits with its widest exponent range (`mpfr_ref.wide`). The golden corpus gains a 910-row edge
  grid, and `complex.adr.tsv` grows to 1,987 rows (row 16: 299).

- **Cyrius port, M1 part 5d-1 — the exact Singularity threshold** (ADR 0002 row 17). The point-charge fields and
  potential, the dipoles, the quadrupole, the Green's functions and `coulomb_force` now test the exact squared distance
  of their inputs against 10^-30 (R against 10^-30 through 10^-60). The new `src/crmath_cmp.cyr` leaves Rust's

  chains with an unbounded exponent) are listed in the port plan §0.1.

- **Cyrius port, M1 part 5d-2 — the vector chains' range edges** (ADR 0002 row 18). `BjFieldVector`'s magnitude,
  magnitude_sq, dot, cross and normalized, the Lorentz force, and the plane and dielectric images run their chains with

### Fixed

- **relativity** (ADR 0002 row 27, M2 part 1d-1): `lienard_wiechert_e` returned Ok(NaN) for a NaN R, which the
  Liénard–Wiechert potentials refuse, and now refuses it with their InvalidParameter ("R vector must not contain
  NaN").
- **wave** (ADR 0002 row 28, M2 part 2a-1, the user's answer of 2026-11-12): `half_wave_dipole_pattern` lost every
  digit below θ ≈ 10^-8 (and near π) to cos((π/2)·cos θ)'s cancellation; its numerator is now sin(π·sin²(θ/2)), or
  sin(π·cos²(θ/2)) where cos θ < 0.
- **circuit** (ADR 0002 row 29, M2 part 2a-1): `rc_charging_voltage` and `rl_current_rise` cancelled in 1 − e^(−t/τ)
  (8e-8 relative at t/τ = 10^-10, 0 below 1.1·10^-16); they now take −expm1(−t/τ), kimiya's correctly rounded expm1
  (`src/crmath_kx.cyr`, with its exp tables in `src/crmath_k_data.cyr`; `cr_verify.py` checks it against MPFR).
- **M2's thresholds** (ADR 0002 row 17 amended, M2 part 2c-1, the user's answers of 2026-11-12): the RC and RL
  transients tested the rounded R·C and L/R against 1e-30, `magnetic_energy_density_material` the rounded μ_r·μ₀,
  `reflectance_normal` and `transmittance_normal` the rounded n₁ + n₂, and the Liénard–Wiechert potentials, E and
  fields the rounded |R|, so a value just below 10^-30 that rounds to the double 1e-30 (which lies above it) passed,
  and one just above could be refused; each is now exact (`src/crmath_cmp.cyr`'s exact sign of a sum of products,
  checked by `cr_verify.py`'s below_* families). `classify_magnetic` reads its −1e-15 as −10^-15 exactly, so
  χ = −1e-15 itself is diamagnetic (Rust: paramagnetic).
- **material** (ADR 0002 row 17 amended, M2 part 2c-2, the user's answer of 2026-11-12): `clausius_mossotti` tested
  the rounded χ = Nα/(3ε₀) against 1 and the rounded ε_r against 0, and computed ε_r = (1 + 2χ)/(1 − χ), which
  cancels at both edges: it refused inputs just below the polarization catastrophe (χ rounding to 1) and just above
  ε_r = 0 (1 + 2χ rounding to 0 where ε_r is about 3e-17), was hundreds of ulps or more off near the pole, and gave
  a NaN payload for a negative Nα past DBL_MAX (the value is −2). Both tests are now exact on Nα, ε_r =
  (3ε₀ + 2Nα)/(3ε₀ − Nα) is correctly rounded, and so is the catastrophe's reported X (new `src/crmath_edge.cyr`:
  double-double sums or exact expansions, kimiya's rounding test, an exact midpoint check; `cr_verify.py`'s cm
  families). X = 1/3 now gives 2.5 exactly (Rust: 2.4999999999999996). The cost: `material/clausius_mossotti`
  26 → 128 ns (`port-parity.md` §37).
- **wave** (ADR 0002 row 17 amended, M2 part 2c-3, the user's answer of 2026-11-12): the trig-free Fresnel family
  computed cos θ_t = √(1 − (n₁/n₂)²(1 − cos²θ_i)) in four roundings and tested the rounded sin²θ_t against 1, so near
  the critical angle it returned cos θ_t = 0 just past it and TIR just short of it, lost cos θ_t near grazing incidence
  for n₁ = n₂ (cos θ_i = 1e-200 gave 0), and gave NaN at normal incidence when n₁/n₂ overflowed. Total internal
  reflection is now n₁²(1 − cos²θ_i) > n₂² exactly, and cos θ_t = √(n₂² − n₁²(1 − cos²θ_i))/n₂ is correctly rounded
  (`src/crmath_edge.cyr`, `cr_verify.py`'s cos_t families), under `snell_cos_theta_t`, the four `*_direct`
  functions, `reflectance_unpolarized`, `transmittance_unpolarized`, `schlick_reflectance` and
  `reflectance_at_interface`. The cost: `wave/fresnel_rs_direct` 61 → 258 ns, `wave/schlick_reflectance` 47 → 244 ns

## [1.2.0] — 2026-11-11

Toolchain/dependency refresh plus a full physics and robustness audit of every module.
No public signatures were changed or removed; APIs whose correct answer did not fit
their signature are deprecated with a correctly-typed replacement. Several functions
now return corrected values or `Err` where they previously returned wrong numbers —
see **Breaking (behavioral)** for the migration notes.

### Breaking (behavioral — no signature changes)

Migration notes for consumers (kiran, joshua, prakash):

- **fdtd**: `ricker_wavelet` is now the true normalized Ricker wavelet (even, peak 1). The previous waveform (first derivative of a Gaussian, peak ≈ 0.858/τ) is available as `differentiated_gaussian` — switch to it to keep old source behavior.
