## Toolchain

- **Cyrius pin**: `6.7.18` (`cyrius.cyml` `[package].cyrius`)
- **ganita**: 1.2.15, from the 6.7.18 stdlib bundle
- **hisab**: none in production (ADR 0001)
- **Rust oracle**: bijli 1.2.0 at `rust-old/`, on rustc 1.99.1. Its transcendentals come from
  the host **glibc 2.44** (Rust std), except `cbrt` (compiler-builtins' correctly rounded port of
  CORE-MATH) and `powi` (compiler-rt's `__powidf2`); its complex arithmetic from Rust hisab
  1.4.0's `Complex`
- **MPFR**: 4.2.2 (system `libmpfr.so.6`), for `validate.py` and the corrected oracle
  (development only)

## Source

- **Rust reference**: `rust-old/`, 22,696 lines in all, of which 8,589 are code in `src/` (no
  tests, comments or blank lines). Frozen, never edited.
- **Cyrius port**: the `src/main.cyr` stub, the generated `src/constants.cyr` (12 public
  constants), `src/mathx.cyr` (the Rust-semantics helpers) and the correctly rounded primitives:
  `src/crmath.cyr` (impetus's, plus bijli's tan, sinh and cosh) and `src/crmath_k.cyr` (kimiya's acos and log10, bijli's asin), with
  their generated tables `src/crmath_data.cyr` and `src/crmath_k_data.cyr`, and `src/crmath_kx.cyr` (kimiya's correctly
  rounded monomials and unbounded exponent, generated by `scripts/port/kimiya_closure.py`), `src/crmath_mono.cyr`
  (bijli's correctly rounded √ of a monomial), `src/crmath_ux.cyr` (bijli's e^x, cosh, sinh, ln, pow, sin and cos
  with an unbounded exponent) and `src/crmath_cmp.cyr` (the exact Singularity threshold); `src/fmtf.cyr` (Rust's f64
  Display, Debug and serde_json's forms), `src/log.cyr`, `src/error.cyr`, `src/complex.cyr`, `src/field.cyr`,
  `src/maxwell.cyr`, `src/charge.cyr` and `src/json.cyr` (on bayan, now a stdlib dependency). The public API so far:
  `BjError`, `BjComplex`, `BjFieldVector`, `BjPolyline`, `BjPointCharge` and their methods, the constants and 95
  public free fns (`docs/api-surface.snapshot`). The golden corpus is in `tests/golden/` (9 modules, 199,781 rows with
  the corrected oracle's 6,523); the bundle is `dist/bijli.cyr`.
