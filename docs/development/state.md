## Toolchain

- **Cyrius pin**: `6.7.18` (`cyrius.cyml` `[package].cyrius`)
- **ganita**: 1.2.15, from the 6.7.18 stdlib bundle
- **hisab**: none in production (ADR 0001)
- **Rust oracle**: bijli 1.2.0 at `rust-old/`, on rustc 1.99.1. Its transcendentals come from
  the host **glibc 2.44** (Rust std), except `cbrt` (compiler-builtins' correctly rounded port of
  CORE-MATH) and `powi` (compiler-rt's `__powidf2`); its complex arithmetic from Rust hisab
  1.4.0's `Complex`
- **MPFR**: 4.2.2 (system `libmpfr.so.6`), for `validate.py` and the corrected oracle
  (development only)

## Source

- **Rust reference**: `rust-old/`, 22,696 lines in all, of which 8,589 are code in `src/` (no
  tests, comments or blank lines). Frozen, never edited.
- **Cyrius port**: the `src/main.cyr` stub, the generated `src/constants.cyr` (12 public
  constants), `src/mathx.cyr` (the Rust-semantics helpers) and the correctly rounded primitives:
  `src/crmath.cyr` (impetus's, plus bijli's tan, sinh and cosh) and `src/crmath_k.cyr` (kimiya's acos and log10, bijli's asin), with
  their generated tables `src/crmath_data.cyr` and `src/crmath_k_data.cyr`; `src/fmtf.cyr` (Rust's f64
  Display and serde_json's form), `src/log.cyr`, `src/error.cyr` and `src/complex.cyr`. The public API so far:
  `BjError`, `BjComplex` and its methods, the constants and 29 public fns (`docs/api-surface.snapshot`). The golden corpus is in
  `tests/golden/` (34,746 rows); the bundle is `dist/bijli.cyr`.
